# One line per factory preset, for the website demos and their blurbs.
#
# shared/tools/make-demos.sh prefers these over the preset's own description,
# which is written as documentation. One "Preset Name = text" per line; blank
# lines and # comments are ignored. Every preset must have a line here, or the
# script falls back and warns.

Kick Duck = The bass dips out of the way of the kick, and nothing above it moves.
Half Speed Stop = One bar drags into half speed and hangs there until the next downbeat.
Filter Wobble = A resonant cutoff sweep on the eighth notes, moving under the bass rather than over it.
Gated Stabs = A hard sixteenth-note chop, with the low end jumping on the off-beats.
Drive Pump = Clean between the hits, saturated on them.
Live Stutter = The top end retriggers in sixteenths while the kick and bass hold the floor.
Crush Steps = A sixteenth-note pattern of bit and sample-rate crush, moving with the beat.
Pan Steps = The bottom end jumps left and right in sixteenths while the rest holds the centre.
Bended Duck = A quarter-note duck that snaps back fast and then eases into unity.
Sidechain Duck = The whole mix breathes around the kick, hit for hit, however the kick is programmed.
